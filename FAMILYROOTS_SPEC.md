# FamilyRoots — Product Vision & Technical Specification

## Overview
**FamilyRoots** is a premium, private digital family relationship and interactive family tree management SaaS platform.

## Core Features
1. **Interactive Family Tree Visualizer**:
   - Canvas/SVG graph rendering multi-generational family networks.
   - 5 Layout modes: Tree, Horizontal, Vertical, Compact, Relationship Graph.
   - Zoom, Pan, Drag-and-drop, Search focus, Node side drawer.

2. **Comprehensive Relationship Engine**:
   - Supports parent-child, spouse, sibling, uncle, aunt, cousin, nephew, niece, in-laws, and custom relationships.

3. **Complete SaaS Suite**:
   - **Dashboard**: Stats, mini-tree widget, upcoming events, recent family activity, recently added members carousel.
   - **My Family & Members**: Grid view, List view, Tree view, relationship group filters.
   - **Add Family Member Flow**: Multi-step modal (Relationship selector, Personal bio, Cross-member connections, Privacy).
   - **Person Profile**: Comprehensive timeline, relationships, memories, events, documents.
   - **Memories**: Media gallery, tagged members, story mode, comments.
   - **Events**: Interactive family calendar (Month, Week, List), RSVP status.
   - **Documents**: Secure privacy-first vault with access control badges.
   - **Invitations & Notifications**: Sent/Received invitations tab, notification drawer.
   - **Settings & Permissions**: Granular access control, role management (Owner, Admin, Member, Viewer).
